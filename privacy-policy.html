PaperGrid Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Package ID: com.papergrid.puzzles

PaperGrid is designed as an offline-first logic puzzle app that respects your privacy. You do not need to create an account, enter your name, or share an email address to play.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your puzzle progress, solve times, daily streaks, theme preferences, language setting, and Pro entitlement status are stored locally on your device using standard app storage. This data is never uploaded to PaperGrid servers because PaperGrid operates with no backend servers.

2. Permissions

PaperGrid does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or external files. On Android, vibration permission is used strictly for optional tactile haptic feedback, and network access is used only when online to display optional rewarded/interstitial ads or verify in-app purchases via Google Play.

3. Advertising & In-App Purchases (When Enabled)

Free versions of PaperGrid may display optional rewarded video hints or light interstitial breaks through Google AdMob when your device is online. Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers and diagnostics in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. One-time Pro purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store; PaperGrid never receives or stores your payment card details.

4. Children’s Privacy

PaperGrid is intended for general audiences (ages 13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or support, please reach out via the developer contact email listed on the PaperGrid Google Play Store page.
